# Legal handoff before public launch

The customer-facing Privacy Policy, Terms and Security pages contain usable product copy, but they are **not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal review**.

Before a public paid launch, the operator should confirm with counsel at minimum:

- the legal entity and registered contact details;
- governing law, liability and termination terms;
- GDPR/UK GDPR or other privacy disclosures that apply to target customers;
- retention periods, subprocessors and international data transfers;
- payment-services positioning and wording based on the actual licensed PSP arrangement;
- sanctions/AML/KYC responsibilities between FlowPay and payment partners;
- pricing, refund and dispute terms once subscriptions or transaction fees are enabled.

Do not advertise FlowPay as a bank, payment institution, money transmitter, custodian, guaranteed FX venue or regulated payment executor unless the corresponding legal basis is actually in place.
